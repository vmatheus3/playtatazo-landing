Ways to play

Six worlds. Pick your adventure.

Every world is the same valley with its own rules. Choose one when you make a world, or keep a few: your gator's progress in each one stays its own.

ExploreArt

Roam without a fight

No levels, and creatures never attack. Craft gear others must hunt for, and fill a Discovery journal of every land.

  • No levels
  • Creatures never attack
  • Nothing lost

Best for: young explorers and families.

SurvivalArt

A world that pushes back

Hunger, thirst, cold and heat, food that spoils and gear that wears. Light a fire, build a shelter and hold out till dawn.

  • Thirst and hunger
  • Cold and heat
  • Graves and worn gear

Best for: older players who love a challenge.

CozyArt

Slow days by the river

Gentle creatures that only fight back if you start it, half the damage, double XP and longer days. Farm, fish and take it easy.

  • No hunger
  • Double XP
  • Nothing lost

Best for: little ones and first-timers.

CreativeArt

Build anything

Nothing can hurt you, trees and rocks come down in one hit, and you can even build in the village.

  • No damage
  • One-hit gathering
  • No lava, no drowning

Best for: builders with a big idea.

For parents and carers

Made with families in mind

Here is what TATAZO does to keep play kind, private and fair, and the controls grown-ups get. It is all in plain words in the family guide.

Read the family guide

No ads, no trackers

No advertising and no third-party analytics, in the game or on this site.

No loot boxes

Nothing is random for money, there are no timers, and coins are earned only by playing.

Your build is protected

A Home Totem guards what you build. Only friends you add can build, dig or open your chests.

Controls for grown-ups

A Family screen behind a parent PIN: daily play time, trading, friend approval, break reminders, and purchases that stay off until you turn them on.

No free-text chat

Players talk with friendly preset phrases and emotes. Nobody can type a message to your child, and anyone can be muted or blocked.

Worlds for everyone

Every world says who it is for. A child's profile only lists worlds for everyone, and a host can lock a world with a password.

No game can promise nothing will ever go wrong. The best protection is still a grown-up who plays along, so TATAZO is built to be fun to play together.
